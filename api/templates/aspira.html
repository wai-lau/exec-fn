Polygons spiral in toward your core along twelve paths. Build towers inside the central ring; they fire outward at the lanes. Credits you hold when a wave is sent earn interest.

Build

Tap a tower type, then tap open ground. Tap a built tower to upgrade, sell or retarget. Keys: U upgrade, 1 2 3 speed, M sound, space pause, tab next wave, Esc cancel.
